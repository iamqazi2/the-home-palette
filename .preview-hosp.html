From Your Vision to Your Table

Have a Design in Mind? We'll Help You Bring It to Life

Whether you are launching a new restaurant, refreshing an existing concept or looking for a signature collection, our team works with you from concept and design through to production and delivery.

It starts with your table, not our catalogue

01
  1. 01

    Consultation

    Tell us the concept, the cover count and the budget. We look at your menu, your interiors and how your team actually plates, then recommend shapes and finishes that will hold up in your service.

    Free of charge
  2. 02

    Design & colour matching

    We match glazes to your brand palette, adjust rim profiles and sizes, and lay out the full setting — plate, bowl, cup, platter — so you can see the table before anything is fired.

    14 colour families
  3. 03

    Sampling & approval

    Physical samples reach your hands before the production run. Weight, stack height, dishwasher behaviour and thermal shock all get checked against real service conditions, and nothing scales up until you sign off.

    Approved by you
  4. 04

    Production & delivery

    We schedule the run against your opening or reorder date and deliver nationwide, packed to survive the journey — with every future reorder matched back to your master sample.

    Nationwide
